# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 24, 2026

Mands IT BD ("we," "us," or "our") operates the **Class Time Pro** mobile application (including the Free and Paid versions). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

### Types of Data Collected

#### Personal Data
While using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you ("Personal Data"). Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:
*   Student Name (stored locally)
*   Institution Name, Department, and Academic Year (stored locally)
*   Email address (only if signing in via Google Auth)

#### Local Storage & Offline Usage
**Class Time Pro** stores your academic timetables, class schedules, assignments, tasks, exams, and attendance records locally on your device using **Hive** local storage. 
*   **Free Version:** Data is stored locally on the device.
*   **Paid Version:** Data is stored locally on the device, with options to backup and sync configurations via cloud storage.

No academic data is uploaded to remote servers without your explicit request or consent.

---

## 2. Push Notifications & Local Reminders
Our application uses local notification scheduling to remind you of upcoming classes and exams.
*   **Permissions:** You can enable or disable these notifications at any time via the in-app settings panel or your device's operating system settings.
*   **Third-Party SDKs:** We integrate **OneSignal** for remote notification broadcasts. OneSignal may collect unique device identifiers to deliver push messages.

## 3. Third-Party Service Providers
We may employ third-party companies and individuals to facilitate our Service, perform Service-related services, or assist us in analyzing how our Service is used. These third parties have access to your Personal Data only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

The application integrates the following third-party services:
*   **Google Play Services** (Core application support)
*   **Firebase Authentication** (Optional account login)
*   **Google Sign-In** (Optional cloud sync)
*   **OneSignal** (Push Notification broadcasts)

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our Service is designed for students of various age groups. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us so we can take immediate action to remove the data from our local/remote services.

The security of your data is important to us. Remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.
